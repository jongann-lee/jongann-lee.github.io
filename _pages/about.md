I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I work with [Prof. Melkior Ornik](https://mornik.web.illinois.edu/) in the [LEADCAT group](https://mornik.web.illinois.edu/research/group/). My research interests are robot learning, multi-agent planning, and geometric learning. I currently work on multi-agent planning in uncertain environments and point cloud foundation models for robotics.

I'm actively looking for research internship positions in robotics, 3D computer vision, and path planning for summer 2027!
